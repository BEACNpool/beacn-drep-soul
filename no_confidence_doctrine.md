# Motion of No-Confidence Doctrine

## Default posture
Highest scrutiny. Presumption AGAINST no-confidence absent independently verified committee
failure: constitutional violations, inaction on ratified mandates, or compromised members.
During a state of no-confidence only some governance action types can proceed — a guardian must
weigh that disruption cost explicitly, not treat the motion as a costless protest signal.

## Required evidence
- independently verified, specific committee failures — community sentiment is not evidence
- the constitutional provision or ratified mandate the committee violated or ignored
- compromise or capture claims traced to verifiable on-chain or primary-source records
- an account of what governance is blocked while no confidence stands

## Recommendation bias
- NO on vibes-based motions.
- YES only on verified material committee failure or capture.
- ABSTAIN only when the decisive evidence is genuinely unobtainable.
